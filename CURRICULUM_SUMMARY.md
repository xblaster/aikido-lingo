## Icon System (BLACK AND WHITE ONLY)

### Critical Requirement
**ALL icons must be BLACK (#000000) with NO other colors**. This ensures:
- Visual consistency across the application
- High contrast for accessibility
- Clear, professional appearance
- No distraction from learning content

### Primary Icon Type: Lucide React Icons

Using lucide-react library provides:
- Extensive icon library (1000+ icons)
- Consistent stroke-based design
- Perfect for monochrome rendering
- TypeScript support
- Scalable and responsive

### Icon Categories

#### 1. People & Positions
```typescript
User, Users, UserCircle, UserCheck, UserX
```
**Usage**: Representing practitioners, correct/incorrect positions

#### 2. Directions & Movement
```typescript
ArrowUp, ArrowDown, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight
ArrowUpRight, ArrowDownLeft (diagonals)
RotateCw, RotateCcw (rotation/tenkan)
Repeat, RefreshCw (continuous movement)
Move, MoveHorizontal, MoveVertical
```
**Usage**: Movement directions, tai sabaki, technique flow

#### 3. Shapes & Structure
```typescript
Circle, Square, Triangle
Minimize2, Maximize2 (spacing/distance)
```
**Usage**: Geometric positions, ma-ai, structural concepts

#### 4. Actions & Techniques
```typescript
Hand, Grip (grabbing/control)
Target, Crosshair (striking points/atemi)
Lock, Unlock (immobilizations)
Link, Unlink (connection/musubi)
```
**Usage**: Technique applications, controls, connections

#### 5. Safety & Awareness
```typescript
Shield, ShieldAlert
AlertTriangle
Eye, EyeOff
```
**Usage**: Safety-critical content, awareness principles

#### 6. Learning & Progression
```typescript
Book, BookOpen
GraduationCap
TrendingUp
CheckCircle, XCircle
```
**Usage**: Learning units, progression, assessment

#### 7. Weapons
```typescript
Sword (bokken/ken)
Minus (jo - simple line representation)
GitBranch (tanto/knife)
```
**Usage**: Weapons identification and techniques

#### 8. Principles
```typescript
Wind, Waves (ki, kokyu, flow)
Gauge (pressure, control measurement)
Zap (energy/ki)
```
**Usage**: Abstract concepts, energy, breath power

## Exercise Distribution Guidelines

### Per Unit Composition (8-12 exercises)

| Exercise Type | Percentage | Purpose |
|--------------|------------|---------|
| **Term to Icon** | 35% | Visual recognition, associating terms with icons |
| **Icon to Term** | 35% | Term recall, reverse association |
| **Safety Quiz** | 15-30% | Critical safety knowledge (more in safety-critical units) |
| **Free Recall** | 15% | Deep learning, self-assessment |

### Difficulty Progression Within Unit

1. **Exercises 1-2**: Difficulty 1 (easiest terms, direct matching)
2. **Exercises 3-6**: Difficulty 2 (core content, standard recognition)
3. **Exercises 7-9**: Difficulty 3 (similar terms requiring discrimination)
4. **Exercises 10+**: Mix of Free Recall and Safety Quiz (integration)

### Safety Quiz Requirements

**Minimum per unit**:
- Regular units: 1 safety quiz
- Safety-critical units: 2-4 safety quizzes

**Focus areas**:
- Injury prevention techniques
- Partner communication protocols
- Equipment handling procedures
- Dojo safety rules
- Proper progression methods

## Pedagogical Principles

### 1. Safety-First Approach
- All safety-critical content introduced early (5th kyu)
- Progressive risk introduction (seated → standing → throws)
- Constant reinforcement of tap signals and partner awareness
- Anatomical understanding for joint locks

### 2. Spiral Curriculum
- Concepts revisited at increasing complexity
- Terms reappear in multiple contexts across belt levels
- Each belt level reinforces previous learning
- Example: Ikkyo appears in white 5, refined in white 4, varied in yellow 3

### 3. Balanced Content Distribution

| Category | Percentage | Focus |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Techniques (Waza)** | 40% | Core aikido techniques |
| **Movements (Tai Sabaki)** | 20% | Body movement principles |
| **Positions (Kamae)** | 15% | Stances and postures |
| **Weapons (Buki Waza)** | 10% | Traditional weapons |
| **Philosophy (Principles)** | 10% | Deeper concepts |
| **Etiquette (Reigi)** | 5% | Dojo conduct |

### 4. Cultural Authenticity
- Correct Japanese terminology (not anglicized)
- Hepburn romanization standard
- Cultural context explained where relevant
- Respect for aikido's traditional roots
- Modern application balanced with tradition

### 5. Progressive Challenge
- Difficulty increases within and across belt levels
- Each belt builds systematically on previous knowledge
- Higher standards for advanced levels (black belt: 85% vs 80%)
- Vocabulary complexity increases gradually
