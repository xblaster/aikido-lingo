# Complete Aikido Curriculum Design
## 5th Kyu (White Belt) to 1st Dan (Black Belt)

This document outlines the complete pedagogical progression for the Aikido Lingo application.

---

## Curriculum Overview

### Belt Progression Structure

1. **5th Kyu (White Belt)** - Fundamentals & Safety
   - 5 units: Etiquette, Basic Positions, Movement Foundations, Safety & Ukemi Basics, First Techniques

2. **4th Kyu (White Belt)** - Building Foundations
   - 5 units: Advanced Positions, Tai Sabaki, Ikkyo Introduction, Weapons Awareness, Ukemi Development

3. **3rd Kyu (Yellow Belt)** - Core Techniques Begin
   - 6 units: Pinning Techniques 1-2, Tenkan Mastery, Irimi Techniques, Kokyu Principles, Weapons Integration

4. **2nd Kyu (Yellow Belt)** - Technical Expansion
   - 6 units: Pinning Techniques 3-4, Projection Techniques, Advanced Footwork, Jo Introduction, Partner Dynamics

5. **1st Kyu (Orange Belt)** - Pre-Black Belt Mastery
   - 6 units: Advanced Projections, Henka Waza, Kaeshi Waza, Weapons Combinations, Philosophy Integration, Synthesis

6. **1st Dan (Black Belt)** - First Teaching Level
   - 5 units: Teaching Fundamentals, Advanced Applications, Jiyu Waza, Weapons Mastery, O-Sensei's Vision

---

## Pedagogical Principles

### Safety-First Approach
- All safety-critical content introduced in 5th kyu
- Safety quizzes in every belt level
- Progressive introduction of risk (tatami → standing → throws)

### Spiral Curriculum
- Concepts revisited at increasing complexity
- Terms reappear in multiple contexts
- Each belt reinforces previous learning

### Balanced Content Distribution
- 40% Techniques (waza)
- 20% Movements (tai sabaki)
- 15% Positions (kamae)
- 10% Weapons (buki waza)
- 10% Philosophy (principles)
- 5% Etiquette (reigi)

### Icon Design Philosophy
**CRITICAL: ALL ICONS MUST BE BLACK AND WHITE ONLY**
- Use lucide-react icons with black color (#000000)
- Simple stick figures for persons
- Clear directional arrows
- High contrast for visibility
- No gradients, no colors, pure monochrome

## Detailed Belt Breakdown

### 5th Kyu (White Belt) - 5 Units

#### Unit 1: Dojo Etiquette (Already implemented)
- Focus: Respect, conduct, basic terminology
- 10 terms: rei, seiza, dojo, sensei, uke, tori, tatami, onegaishimasu, arigatou, kamiza
- Safety: Dojo entry/exit, bowing protocol

#### Unit 2: Basic Positions
- Focus: Foundational stances
- 12 terms: shizentai, kamae, hanmi, migi hanmi, hidari hanmi, gyaku hanmi, ai hanmi, shikko, suwari waza, tachi waza, hanza handachi
- Safety: Proper knee positioning, weight distribution

#### Unit 3: Movement Foundations
- Focus: Basic footwork and spatial awareness
- 10 terms: ayumi ashi, tsugi ashi, tenkan, irimi, ma-ai, issoku ittou no ma-ai, to-ma, chika-ma, tai sabaki, happo giri
- Safety: Maintaining balance, spatial awareness

#### Unit 4: Safety & Ukemi Basics
- Focus: Safe falling and rolling (SAFETY-CRITICAL)
- 12 terms: ukemi, mae ukemi, ushiro ukemi, yoko ukemi, zempo kaiten, koho tento, shomen, yokomen, tobi ukemi, ushiro ukemi from standing
- Safety: Proper head protection, slapping technique, progressive height

#### Unit 5: First Techniques
- Focus: Introduction to basic pins and controls
- 10 terms: ikkyo, katate dori, ryote dori, kata dori, shomen uchi, yokomen uchi, munetsuki, ushiro ryote dori, kihon waza, nagare
- Safety: Controlled speed, tap-out signal

---

### 4th Kyu (White Belt Advanced) - 5 Units

#### Unit 1: Advanced Kamae
- Focus: Refined positioning and connection
- 10 terms: sankaku no kamae, maai no kamae, chudan no kamae, jodan no kamae, gedan no kamae, tegatana, furi kaburi, musubi, awase, kokyu
- Safety: Hand blade safety, maintaining connection

#### Unit 2: Tai Sabaki Deep Dive
- Focus: Advanced body movement principles
- 12 terms: tenshin, tai no henko, morote dori kokyu ho, kokyu ryoku, ki no nagare, aiki, chushin, kuzushi, tsukuri, kake, zanshin, metsuke
- Safety: Maintaining stability during turns

#### Unit 3: Ikkyo Variations
- Focus: First principle applied in different attacks
- 10 terms: ikkyo omote, ikkyo ura, suwari waza ikkyo, tachi waza ikkyo, hanmi handachi ikkyo, ushiro waza, osae waza, katame waza, kansetsu waza, taisabaki ikkyo
- Safety: Joint locks limits, communication with partner

#### Unit 4: Weapons Awareness
- Focus: Introduction to traditional weapons
- 12 terms: bokken, ken, tanto, jo, buki waza, aiki ken, aiki jo, ken tai jo, kumitachi, kumijo, suburi, happo giri ken
- Safety: Weapons handling, distance with weapons, never throw real weapons

#### Unit 5: Ukemi Development
- Focus: More dynamic falling
- 10 terms: kaiten nage ukemi, koshi nage ukemi, juji garami ukemi, ude garami ukemi, high falls progression, multiple ukemi, continuous ukemi, flowing ukemi
- Safety: Progressive height increase, spotting technique

---

### 3rd Kyu (Yellow Belt) - 6 Units

#### Unit 1: Nikyo - Second Principle
- Focus: Wrist control technique
- 10 terms: nikyo, nikyo omote, nikyo ura, nikkajo, kote mawashi, tekubi osae, kotegaeshi preparation, sankajo foundation
- Safety: Wrist sensitivity, gradual pressure application

#### Unit 2: Sankyo - Third Principle
- Focus: Spiral wrist control
- 10 terms: sankyo, sankyo omote, sankyo ura, sankajo, kote hineri, spiral motion, entering for sankyo, transitioning to pin
- Safety: Dangerous if applied suddenly, requires smooth entry

#### Unit 3: Tenkan Mastery
- Focus: Pivot and circular movement
- 12 terms: tenkan undo, tenkan ho, tenkan nage, kaiten, mawari, meguri, soto kaiten, uchi kaiten, tai no tenkan, ushiro tenkan
- Safety: Partner's balance during pivots

#### Unit 4: Irimi Techniques
- Focus: Entering movements and throws
- 10 terms: irimi nage, irimi nage omote, irimi nage ura, kokyu nage, kokyu ho, juji garami, gokyo preparation, aiki nage
- Safety: Neck safety in irimi nage, controlled throwing

#### Unit 5: Kokyu Principles
- Focus: Breath power and timing
- 10 terms: kokyu ryoku, kokyu dosa, kokyu nage, aiki age, breath timing, extension vs contraction, soft power, ju
- Safety: Not using muscle strength, protecting partner

#### Unit 6: Weapons Integration
- Focus: Empty hand vs weapon defenses
- 12 terms: tanto dori, tachi dori, jo dori, hanmi handachi with weapons, disarming principles, maai with weapons, kumitachi basics
- Safety: CRITICAL - weapon safety, distance, never lose control of weapon

---

### 2nd Kyu (Yellow Belt Advanced) - 6 Units

#### Unit 1: Yonkyo - Fourth Principle
- Focus: Pressure point control
- 10 terms: yonkyo, yonkyo omote, yonkyo ura, yonkajo, tekubi pressure point, nerve technique, pain compliance, kote hineri to yonkyo
- Safety: Requires precise point, can cause injury if misapplied

#### Unit 2: Gokyo - Fifth Principle
- Focus: Arm bar control
- 10 terms: gokyo, gokyo omote, gokyo ura, gokajo, ude nobashi, arm extension control, weapon retention, tanto dori finish
- Safety: Elbow hyperextension risk, controlled application

#### Unit 3: Projection Techniques (Nage Waza)
- Focus: Throwing principles
- 12 terms: shiho nage, shiho nage omote, shiho nage ura, kote gaeshi, kote gaeshi omote, kote gaeshi ura, kaiten nage, kaiten nage uchi, kaiten nage soto, tenchi nage
- Safety: Proper ukemi essential, breakfall training, spotting

#### Unit 4: Advanced Footwork
- Focus: Precise movement patterns
- 10 terms: suri ashi, okuri ashi, tsugi ashi variations, hiraki ashi, yori ashi, de ashi, tsukuri in movement
- Safety: Maintaining posture, not crossing feet dangerously

#### Unit 5: Jo Fundamentals
- Focus: Staff techniques and kata
- 12 terms: jo suburi, hasso, gedan gaeshi, ushiro tsuki, kaeshi tsuki, honte uchi, gyakute uchi, 13 jo kata, 31 jo kata
- Safety: Jo distance, never lose grip, weapon control

#### Unit 6: Partner Dynamics
- Focus: Advanced interaction principles
- 10 terms: ki musubi, ai uchi, deai, go no sen, sen no sen, sen sen no sen, maai timing, awase timing
- Safety: Training intensity levels, respecting partner limits

---

### 1st Kyu (Orange Belt) - 6 Units

#### Unit 1: Advanced Projections
- Focus: Complex throwing techniques
- 12 terms: juji garami omote/ura, ude garami, hiji kime osae, ude gaeshi, aiki otoshi, sumi otoshi, koshi nage, soto kaiten nage, uchi kaiten nage
- Safety: CRITICAL - high impact throws, requires excellent ukemi

#### Unit 2: Henka Waza (Variations)
- Focus: Technical adaptations
- 10 terms: henka, oyo waza, applied variations, ikkyo to nikyo transition, nikyo to sankyo flow, sankyo to yonkyo, gokyo to nage waza
- Safety: Smooth transitions prevent injury

#### Unit 3: Kaeshi Waza (Counters)
- Focus: Reversals and counters
- 10 terms: kaeshi waza, uke's counter, tori's counter to counter, mutual technique, aiki age counter, flowing reversal
- Safety: Both partners must be skilled, slow practice essential

#### Unit 4: Weapons Combinations
- Focus: Multi-weapon kata and kumitachi
- 12 terms: ki musubi no tachi, 1-5 kumitachi, 1-5 kumijo, awase, disarming sequences, tanto vs empty hand advanced
- Safety: Partner awareness with weapons, distance critical

#### Unit 5: Philosophy Integration
- Focus: Deeper principles and history
- 12 terms: aiki, ki, kokyu, takemusu aiki, masakatsu agatsu, bu, budo, do, riai, omote, ura, hyoshi
- Safety: Mental discipline, ego management

#### Unit 6: Synthesis and Flow
- Focus: Putting it all together
- 10 terms: jiyu waza preparation, randori, kakari geiko, ki no nagare application, continuous technique, multiple attackers basics
- Safety: Controlled chaos, clear communication

---

### 1st Dan (Black Belt) - 5 Units

#### Unit 1: Teaching Fundamentals
- Focus: Pedagogy and demonstration
- 10 terms: teaching principles, demonstration technique, correction methods, ukemi for teaching, safe progression, student assessment
- Safety: Teaching safety protocols

#### Unit 2: Advanced Applications
- Focus: Real-world adaptation
- 10 terms: tanto dori advanced, tachi dori advanced, multiple attack principles, seated defenses, ground defenses adapted
- Safety: Reality vs training, legal considerations

#### Unit 3: Jiyu Waza (Free Technique)
- Focus: Spontaneous application
- 10 terms: jiyu waza, randori, multiple attackers, continuous flow, taninzu gake, calling attacks, freestyle response
- Safety: Controlled intensity, respect in chaos

#### Unit 4: Weapons Mastery
- Focus: Advanced weapons work
- 12 terms: advanced kumitachi, advanced kumijo, ken vs jo, tanto vs ken, weapon disarms mastery, weapons kata advanced
- Safety: High-level weapon control required

#### Unit 5: O-Sensei's Vision
- Focus: Philosophy and purpose
- 10 terms: O-Sensei's teachings, aikido history, bu and peace, harmony principle, ai ki integration, modern aikido, lineage awareness
- Safety: Mental/spiritual safety, avoiding dogma

## Icon Strategy (BLACK AND WHITE ONLY)

### Icon Categories

1. **Person Icons** (stick figures, black only)
   - Standing person: simple vertical line with circle head
   - Kneeling person: bent form
   - Moving person: angled lines
   - Two people: side by side figures

2. **Direction Arrows** (black arrows)
   - Straight: up, down, left, right
   - Curved: rotation indicators
   - Spiral: tenkan movements
   - Double: both directions

3. **Geometric Shapes** (black outlines)
   - Triangle: hanmi stance base
   - Circle: circular movements
   - Square: stability
   - Lines: directional indicators

4. **Weapon Icons** (black silhouettes)
   - Bokken: straight sword shape
   - Jo: staff line
   - Tanto: short blade
   - Combined: crossed weapons

5. **Hand/Body Part Icons** (black line drawings)
   - Open hand: tegatana
   - Fist: striking
   - Wrist: joint locks
   - Knee: suwari waza

### Lucide Icons to Use (all black)
- User, Users, UserCircle (people)
- ArrowUp, ArrowDown, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight (directions)
- RotateCw, RotateCcw (tenkan)
- Repeat, RefreshCw (continuous movement)
- Circle, Square, Triangle (shapes)
- Hand, Grip (grabs)
- Shield, ShieldAlert (safety)
- Book, BookOpen (learning)
- Target, Crosshair (focus/aim)
- Footprints (tai sabaki)

## Exercise Distribution Guidelines

### Per Unit Composition (8-12 exercises)
- 35% Term to Icon (visual recognition)
- 35% Icon to Term (term recall)
- 15% Safety Quiz (critical knowledge)
- 15% Free Recall (deep learning)

### Difficulty Progression Within Unit
1. First 2 exercises: Difficulty 1 (easiest terms)
2. Exercises 3-6: Difficulty 2 (core terms)
3. Exercises 7-9: Difficulty 3 (similar terms, requires discrimination)
4. Last 2-3 exercises: Mix of Free Recall and Safety Quiz

### Safety Quiz Integration
- Minimum 1 per unit (2-3 for safety-critical units)
- Focus on injury prevention
- Emphasize partner respect
- Cover dojo protocols
